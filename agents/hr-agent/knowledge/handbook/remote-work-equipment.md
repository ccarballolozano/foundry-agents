# MyConcepts Remote Work: Equipment and Allowance

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

This document explains the equipment and allowance for working from home. What the benefit is and who is eligible are described in the benefit catalog.

## One-off home office budget

- Up to **EUR 400** once, for a desk, chair, monitor, or headset.
- Order through the equipment portal **KitShop**, or buy yourself and claim with the receipt in MyConcepts People (Expenses → Home office) within **30 days**.
- Equipment bought with this budget stays yours after **24 months**. If you leave earlier, you can return it or pay its remaining value.

## Monthly allowance

- **EUR 30 per month** towards internet and energy costs, paid with your salary.
- The allowance is paid automatically once your remote work arrangement is registered in MyConcepts People.

## Setting up your arrangement

1. Agree your remote days with your manager.
2. Register the arrangement in **MyConcepts People** (Work location → Remote arrangement).
3. Complete the "Healthy home workplace" self-check in MyConcepts Learn within **2 weeks**.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for up to **20 working days per year**, with approval from your manager and HR at least **4 weeks** in advance.
