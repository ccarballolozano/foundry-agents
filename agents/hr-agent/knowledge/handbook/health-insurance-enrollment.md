# MyConcepts Health Insurance: Enrollment and Changes

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

This document explains how to enroll in and change your health insurance. What the plan covers and who is eligible are described in the benefit catalog.

## Insurer

The group plan is provided by the fictional insurer **SaludPlus**.

## Enrolling as a new employee

1. Enroll within **30 days** of your start date in **MyConcepts People** (Benefits → Health insurance).
2. Coverage starts on the **first day of the month** after you enroll.
3. You receive your SaludPlus card by post within **10 working days**.

If you miss the 30-day window, you can enroll at the next open enrollment period.

## Adding dependents

- Add your partner or children in MyConcepts People and upload proof of relationship (for example, a family book or birth certificate).
- A newborn or newly adopted child must be added within **60 days** of the birth or adoption.
- MyConcepts pays the full premium for the employee. For each dependent, the employee pays **EUR 25 per month**, deducted from salary.

## Open enrollment

Open enrollment runs every year from **1 to 30 November**. Changes made then take effect on **1 January**.

## Life events

You can change your plan outside open enrollment within **30 days** of a life event: marriage or civil partnership, birth or adoption, divorce, or your partner losing their own coverage.
