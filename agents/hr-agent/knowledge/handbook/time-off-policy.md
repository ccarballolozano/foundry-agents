# MyConcepts Time-Off Policy

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

## Annual holiday allowance

- Full-time employees get **25 working days** of paid holiday per calendar year.
- Part-time employees get a pro-rated allowance, rounded up to the nearest half day.
- Employees who join or leave during the year get a pro-rated allowance for the months they work.
- On top of the holiday allowance, MyConcepts observes the public holidays of the office the employee is assigned to.

## Requesting time off

1. Submit the request in **MyConcepts People** (Time off → New request).
2. Give at least **10 working days' notice** for absences of 5 days or more, and **2 working days' notice** for shorter absences.
3. Your manager approves or declines within **3 working days**. Requests that are not answered in that time are escalated to HR automatically.
4. Requests for more than **15 consecutive working days** also need approval from your department head.

## Carry-over

- You can carry over up to **5 unused holiday days** into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the next year; any remaining carried-over days expire after that date.
- Days above the carry-over limit are not paid out, except when an employee leaves MyConcepts (see "Leaving the company").

## Sick leave

- Report a sick day to your manager before **10:00** on the first day of absence.
- From the **4th consecutive day** of sickness, upload a medical certificate in MyConcepts People.
- Sick days do not reduce your holiday allowance.

## Other paid leave

| Reason | Paid days |
|---|---|
| Moving house | 1 day per year |
| Own wedding or civil partnership | 10 days |
| Death of a close family member | 3 days (5 if travel is needed) |
| Volunteering day | 1 day per year |

## Leaving the company

Unused holiday days, including carried-over days, are paid out in the final payslip.
