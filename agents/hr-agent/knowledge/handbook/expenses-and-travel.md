# MyConcepts Expenses and Business Travel

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

## Before you travel

1. Get your manager's approval in **MyConcepts People** (Travel → New trip) before booking.
2. Book flights, trains and hotels through the travel portal **TravelDesk**. Bookings made outside TravelDesk are reimbursed only if they cost less than the TravelDesk offer.
3. Trips outside the European Union also need approval from your department head.

## Travel class

- Trains: standard class; first class for journeys longer than **4 hours**.
- Flights: economy class; premium economy for flights longer than **6 hours**.
- Taxis: allowed when public transport is not available or when travelling between **22:00 and 06:00**.

## Daily limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel (per night, Europe) | EUR 150 |
| Hotel (per night, rest of the world) | EUR 200 |
| Meals (per day, all meals combined) | EUR 55 |
| Mileage with a private car | EUR 0.26 per km |

Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Submitting an expense claim

1. Photograph each receipt and upload it in **MyConcepts People** (Expenses → New claim) within **30 days** of the expense.
2. Choose the matching category and add the project code.
3. Your manager approves the claim, and Finance pays it with the next payroll run that is at least **5 working days** after approval.

Claims submitted more than **90 days** after the expense are not reimbursed.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than **6 times a year** can request a corporate card from Finance. Card expenses must still be justified with receipts within 30 days.
