# MyConcepts Onboarding Checklist

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

Onboarding lasts **4 weeks**. Your manager and an onboarding buddy guide you through it.

## Before your first day

- Sign your contract electronically and return the personal data form.
- HR sends your laptop to your home address, or it is ready at your office desk.

## Week 1

1. Collect your badge at reception and attend the welcome session (Monday, 09:30).
2. Activate your account and set up multi-factor authentication.
3. Complete the mandatory training in **MyConcepts Learn**: information security, data protection, and code of conduct. All three must be finished by the end of week 1.
4. Meet your onboarding buddy.

## Week 2

1. Agree your first-quarter goals with your manager.
2. Set up your profile and emergency contact in **MyConcepts People**.
3. Join your team's recurring meetings and channels.

## Weeks 3 and 4

1. Shadow at least one project or customer meeting.
2. Complete the role-specific learning path in MyConcepts Learn.
3. Have the onboarding review meeting with your manager at the end of week 4. Onboarding is complete when your manager signs off in MyConcepts People.

## Probation

The probation period is **6 months**. A mid-probation review happens at **3 months**.

## Contact

Questions about onboarding go to **onboarding@myconcepts.example**.
