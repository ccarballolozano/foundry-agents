# MyConcepts Parental Leave

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

## Leave entitlement

- The birth parent gets **20 weeks** of fully paid leave.
- The second parent, including adoptive and foster parents, gets **16 weeks** of fully paid leave.
- Leave can start up to **4 weeks** before the expected due date or placement date.
- At least **6 weeks** must be taken right after the birth or placement. The rest can be taken in blocks of at least one week within the child's first **12 months**.

## How to apply

1. Tell your manager and HR at least **15 weeks** before the expected due date or placement date.
2. Submit the "Parental leave plan" form in **MyConcepts People** (Leave → Parental leave), listing the blocks you plan to take.
3. Upload the medical certificate with the expected due date, or the adoption or foster placement document.
4. HR confirms the plan in writing within **10 working days**.

You can change your plan once without giving a reason, with at least **4 weeks' notice**.

## Returning to work

- You can return part-time (50% to 80%) for up to **6 months** after your leave, keeping your full-time contract.
- A return-to-work meeting with your manager is scheduled in your first week back.
- Holiday days keep accruing during parental leave.
