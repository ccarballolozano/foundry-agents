# MyConcepts Learning Budget: Claims Procedure

*MyConcepts employee handbook. Fictional company, demo content only.*

This document explains how to use and claim the learning budget. What the benefit is and who is eligible are described in the benefit catalog.

## Annual amount

- The budget is **EUR 1,200 per calendar year**.
- Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
- Certification exam fees count against the budget; one exam retake per year is also covered.

## Getting a course approved

1. Create a learning request in **MyConcepts Learn** (Budget → New request) with the course name, provider, dates and price.
2. Your manager approves the request within **5 working days**.
3. Requests above **EUR 500** for a single item also need approval from your department head.
4. Get approval **before** you pay or register. Items bought without prior approval are not reimbursed.

## Claiming a reimbursement

1. Pay for the course yourself.
2. Within **60 days** of finishing the course, upload the invoice and the certificate of completion (or proof of attendance) in MyConcepts Learn.
3. The reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll after the claim is approved.

## Repayment

If you leave MyConcepts within **6 months** of a reimbursed item that cost more than **EUR 800**, you repay 50% of that item.
